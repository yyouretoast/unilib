Borrowing Information

  • Patrons with a valid SAMS ID card who are eligible to borrow books.
  • Self-checkout units will issue a receipt that should be kept as a reminder of the due date.
  • If assistance is needed, do not hesitate to contact the circulation staff members.
  • You are accountable for items borrowed on your ID. Do not let others use your ID card, or borrow items on behalf of others.
  • For applicable policies, refer to the University Policies page.
